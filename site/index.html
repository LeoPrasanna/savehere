Common questions

A link won't summarise

SaveHere reads the public text at a link — a caption, description or auto-generated captions. If a post has none of those, or the platform blocks the request, there is nothing to summarise and the app says so rather than inventing one. The item is still saved, and you can pull to refresh or re-summarise it later.

I've run out of AI actions for today

Every summary, recipe, workout and library question uses one AI action. Each plan has a daily allowance that resets at midnight UTC. The profile panel shows what today's actions were spent on. SaveHere Pro raises the limit.

How do I cancel SaveHere Pro?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they are cancelled through Apple: Manage your subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are also Apple's — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

Open the profile panel in the app and choose Delete account. It permanently removes your saved items, notes, generated plans and sign-in record straight away. The Privacy Policy explains the two narrow exceptions.

Is my saved content private?

Yes. Your library is only visible to your account. There is no feed, no sharing, no advertising and no third-party analytics in the app.

Should I trust the summaries?

Treat them as a fast recap, not as fact. AI output can be wrong, and it is never medical, financial or safety advice — check the original before acting on anything that matters.
