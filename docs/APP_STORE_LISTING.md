## App Store Description

**Save any Reel, Short, or Post — get an instant AI summary, then turn it into action.**

SaveHere is your AI-powered second brain for short-form content. Paste a link from Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, or LinkedIn and SaveHere extracts the content, generates a bullet-point AI summary with smart tags, and lets you act on it.

**CAPTURE & SUMMARIZE**
• One-tap save from Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, LinkedIn posts, and Facebook reels
• AI bullet summaries + auto tags + auto category (powered by Claude)
• Works in any language; summaries match the source
• Auto-saved personal notes; editable category; re-summarize anytime

**TURN IT INTO ACTION**
• Recipes & checklists — step-by-step instructions from cooking and how-to content
• Workout plans — exercises with sets, reps, and rest, plus a guided session player
• Manual control — AI generates once, then you add, edit, or delete items yourself

**FIND & REDISCOVER**
• Search across titles, tags, summaries, and notes
• Rediscover — resurfaces older saves so nothing gets forgotten
• Ask your library — natural-language questions answered only from what you saved

**WHY SAVEHERE?**
• No more bookmarking links you'll never revisit
• No more screenshots cluttering your camera roll
• No more forgetting that great recipe or workout you saw on TikTok

**YOUR CONTENT, YOUR CONTROL**
• All saves synced to your account across devices
• Per-user AI quota with daily limits to keep costs fair
• Graceful handling of private/login-walled posts (saved as a labeled bookmark)

**FREE TO START**
• 10-day free trial with unlimited saves
• Extend your trial up to 30 days by referring friends
• Pro subscription plans coming soon with higher AI limits and premium features

SaveHere stores links and AI-generated summaries for your personal reference only. All saved content belongs to its original creators.
