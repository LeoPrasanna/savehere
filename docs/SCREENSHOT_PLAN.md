## Design Notes

- **Background:** Use the app's own dark aurora background (#0B0A0F with violet/cyan/pink blobs) as the screenshot canvas background
- **Mockup frames:** Use realistic iPhone 15 Pro Max frames in Natural Titanium or Space Black
- **Text overlay:** White text, bold weights, subtle drop shadow for readability
- **Accent elements:** Small holographic gradient badges, sparkle icons, floating particles
- **Consistency:** All screenshots should feel like they're from the same app family — same background style, same font treatment, same accent colors

## Tools for Generation
- Figma with a phone mockup template
- Screenshot the actual app running in iOS Simulator (iPhone 15 Pro Max)
- Overlay text and arrange in Figma or Canva
- Export at exactly 1290×2796 and 1284×2778
