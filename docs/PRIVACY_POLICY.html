Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

SaveHere ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, password, and optional profile information (first name, last name, nickname). We use Supabase for authentication and secure storage.

1.2 Saved Content

We store the URLs you save, AI-generated summaries, tags, categories, personal notes, and any workout plans or checklists you create. This is your personal library and is tied to your account.

1.3 Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app, including feature usage, error logs, and performance metrics. We use Sentry for error monitoring.

1.4 Device Information

We may collect device type, operating system version, and app version to ensure compatibility and diagnose issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service: To save, summarize, and organize your content
  • AI features: To generate summaries, tags, workout plans, and recipes using Anthropic Claude
  • Improve the app: To understand which features are used and fix bugs
  • Communications: To send essential service notifications (we do not send marketing emails without consent)

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Supabase: For authentication and database storage
  • Anthropic: For AI summary generation (only the text content of your saved links is sent, never your account info)
  • Sentry: For error monitoring and crash reporting
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, JWT authentication, and per-user data isolation. Your saved content is accessible only to you when authenticated.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your saved data in the app
  • Correction: You can edit your profile and saved content anytime
  • Deletion: You can delete individual saves or request full account deletion by contacting us
  • Export: Export your data feature is planned for a future release

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

SaveHere is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Content

SaveHere stores links and summaries of content from third-party platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook). The original content and its creators retain all ownership rights. We do not host or redistribute the actual video or media content.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
